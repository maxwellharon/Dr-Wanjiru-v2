GLP-1 weight loss and your hair, muscle and skin

What is normal, what is not, and how to lose weight without losing the rest of you.

WDr. Wanjiru MD
Plastic & reconstructive surgeon, Upper Hill, Nairobi · Updated 6 Nov 2026

What GLP-1 medicines do

Medicines such as semaglutide and tirzepatide reduce appetite and affect blood-sugar signalling, helping many people lose significant weight when combined with nutrition and movement. They are prescription medicines and need a medical assessment first.

Why hair can shed

Rapid weight loss, like any physical stress, can push more hairs into the resting phase. Shedding appears about two to four months later and is called telogen effluvium. It is usually temporary, and low iron, protein or other nutrients can make it worse.

  • Eat enough protein and a variety of foods
  • Ask about checking iron, vitamin D, B12 and thyroid
  • Aim for a steady, not extreme, pace

Protecting muscle

A meaningful share of weight lost can be lean mass, including muscle. Strength training two to three times a week and adequate protein help protect muscle and metabolism. Your doctor will set targets for you.

Skin and face changes

Faster loss means less time for skin to adapt, and the face can look more hollow. Hydration, a steady pace and sun protection help. Later, tightening or conservative volume restoration can be considered.

Staying safe

  • Use only prescribed, properly sourced medicine. Falsified pens have been reported
  • Report severe or persistent abdominal pain, vomiting or dehydration
  • Do not use if pregnant or planning pregnancy
  • Plan for maintenance. Many people regain weight after stopping without a plan
