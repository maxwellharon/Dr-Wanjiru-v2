Ageing, honestly

“Black don’t crack” is half the story.

Melanin does protect against sun damage, so deep lines often appear later. But dark skin still ages, usually through volume loss in the mid-face, uneven pigment, dullness and loss of firmness.

That changes the plan: less about freezing expression, more about supporting structure, restoring light reflection and keeping tone even, with a lighter hand than the “standard” template.

Safety first

How to choose an injector.

Injectables are medical procedures. These questions protect you wherever you go.

  • Is the person a doctor trained in facial anatomy?
  • Will you see the sealed product, its brand and batch number?
  • Is hyaluronidase (the filler-dissolving antidote) on hand?
  • Do they explain risks, including vascular complications, before you decide?
  • Is it a sterile clinic, not a home, a party or a pop-up?
  • Can they show results on skin tones like yours?
  • Are you given time to think, or pushed to decide on the day?
Call straight away if

after filler you notice severe or increasing pain, skin turning white, grey or mottled, or changes in vision. These can signal a blood-vessel complication that needs urgent treatment.

Questions

Aesthetics FAQ

Will I look frozen or “done”?

Not if the plan is conservative. The goal is to soften what bothers you while keeping natural movement and your own features.

Do fillers work on Black faces?

Yes. The planning differs: structure, volume and proportion are often more relevant than lines, and technique is adjusted to reduce bruising and pigment risks.

How much downtime is there?

Most treatments have little. Botulinum toxin needs none; fillers may bring swelling or bruising for a few days; peels may flake for several.

Can fillers be dissolved?

Hyaluronic acid fillers can be dissolved with hyaluronidase if needed, which is one reason they are widely used.

Is it safe to combine treatments?

Often yes, and sequencing matters. Your plan will say what to do first and what to leave until the skin is ready.
