FAQ

Your questions, answered.

Search or filter by topic. Anything we have missed, ask at your consultation.

What should I do first for a burn?

Cool it under running water for 20 minutes, remove rings and tight clothing, cover loosely with clean cling film or a clean cloth, and get medical help if it is large, deep, on the face, hands, feet, genitals or joints, or caused by chemicals or electricity.

Can burn scars be improved years later?

Yes. Old burn scars can often be softened, released and re-contoured. Timing affects the approach, not whether help is possible.

Will I need skin grafts?

Deep partial-thickness and full-thickness burns often do. Superficial burns heal without them.

What is the difference between a keloid and a hypertrophic scar?

Hypertrophic scars stay within the wound and often settle over time. Keloids grow beyond the wound and rarely settle without treatment.

Is keloid treatment guaranteed?

No treatment can guarantee permanent flattening, but combination plans reduce recurrence and improve comfort and appearance.

Can traction alopecia be reversed?

Early, often yes. Once follicles are scarred, regrowth is not possible and restoration may be considered.

Is a hair transplant suitable for Afro-textured hair?

Yes, with specialised technique and planning for curved follicles and scar risk.

Do I need sunscreen with dark skin?

Yes. It prevents dark marks from worsening, helps melasma and protects scars.

Are chemical peels safe on dark skin?

Superficial peels with proper skin preparation and conservative strength can be safe. Strong peels carry a higher risk of dark or pale marks.

How long do Botox and fillers last?

Botulinum toxin usually lasts about three to four months. Fillers last from several months to over a year, depending on product and area.

Is there downtime?

Usually minimal. Fillers can cause swelling or bruising for a few days.

Who can take GLP-1 medicines?

Adults who meet medical criteria after assessment. They are prescription-only, and some conditions rule them out.

Will I lose muscle or hair?

Some muscle is usually lost with weight loss, and hair shedding can occur. Protein, strength training and nutrient checks reduce both.
